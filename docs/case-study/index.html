Product Management Case Study

Postmeet. — from insight to shipped product, end to end.

An AI product that turns any meeting transcript into owned, dated action items and pushes each one into the owner's calendar and inbox — with zero setup and no OAuth. This is the full product-management record behind it: the market case, the requirements, the research that should follow, the methodology, and an honest post-launch scorecard.

What we're actually talking about
